Attorney Erik S. Schimmelbusch

Professional Background

Erik Schimmelbusch founded Schimmelbusch Law Group PC in 2010 to provide focused legal counsel to clients in Oregon, Washington, California, and Nevada. His practice is centered on estate planning and administration, business succession planning, and charitable planned giving.

Prior to forming his own firm, Erik was a partner at Davis Wright Tremaine LLP in Portland, Oregon, where he practiced for 13 years. He also practiced with Trainor Robertson (now Trainor Fairbrook) in Sacramento, California, from 1995–1997. Erik draws on his deep knowledge of state and federal laws to assist clients with a variety of uniquely complex and diverse issues.

Practice Highlights

  • Traditional wills and trusts, including marital deduction and federal estate tax exemption planning.
  • Business succession planning for privately held and family-owned companies.
  • Post-mortem administration of estates and trusts, including preparation of federal and state tax returns.
  • Gift tax, estate tax, and generation-skipping transfer tax planning and reporting.
  • Charitable gift planning, including charitable remainder trusts, lead trusts, and general advice to nonprofit organizations.
  • Family wealth transfer vehicles, including grantor retained annuity trusts (GRATs) and sales to intentionally defective grantor trusts (IDGTs).
